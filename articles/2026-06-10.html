'Synthetic performers' in ads must be identified as AI as new New York law takes effect

纽约新法生效：广告中的AI"合成表演者"必须标注身份
📰 Associated Press (AP News) ✍️ Kaitlyn Huamani 📅 June 9, 2026

Any advertisements in New York that feature artificial intelligence-generated people in place of actors will now be violating state law if they don't clearly label that they have used a "synthetic performer."

【译】现在，纽约州任何使用人工智能生成的人物替代真人演员的广告，如果没有明确标注使用了"合成表演者"，就违反了州法律。

The law, signed by Gov. Kathy Hochul last December, took effect Tuesday. Her office called it the "first law of its kind in the nation" that will increase transparency as AI-generated performers appear in various forms of media, including social platforms and digital ads.

【译】这项由州长凯西·霍楚尔（Kathy Hochul）去年12月签署的法律于周二正式生效。她的办公室称这是"全美首部此类法律"，在当前AI生成的表演者出现在包括社交平台和数字广告在内的各种媒体形式中的背景下，将提高透明度。

Synthetic performers are defined under state law as "digitally-created media that appear as a real person." The law applies to ads in any medium.

【译】根据州法律，合成表演者被定义为"看起来像真人的数字创建媒体"。该法律适用于任何媒介的广告。

"In New York, we are setting the rules rather than letting AI call the shots," Hochul, a Democrat, said in a statement. The "simple, honest disclosure" required by the law "protects consumers, respects our creative workforce, and keeps New York at the forefront of responsible innovation," she said.

【译】"在纽约，我们正在制定规则，而不是让人工智能主导一切，"民主党人霍楚尔在一份声明中说。该法律要求的"简单、诚实的披露""保护了消费者，尊重了我们的创意劳动力，并使纽约保持在负责任创新的前沿，"她说。

Any ad that fails to "conspicuously disclose" the use of a synthetic performer will be fined $1,000 for the first violation and $5,000 for each subsequent violation.

【译】凡是未"显著披露"使用了合成表演者的广告，首次违规将被处以1000美元罚款，后续每次违规罚款5000美元。

Specific exemptions are laid out in the law, namely that ads for movies, TV shows, streaming content, video games and other works in which synthetic performers are used throughout the entire work are exempt from labeling. The law also does not apply to audio-only ads or ads in which AI is only used for language translation.

【译】法律中列出了具体的豁免情形，即电影、电视节目、流媒体内容、视频游戏以及其他整部作品都使用合成表演者的广告可免于标注。该法律也不适用于纯音频广告，或仅将AI用于语言翻译的广告。

Last year as the legislation moved through the state Legislature, the American Association of Advertising Agencies — better known as the 4A's — and several other advertising organizations put out a statement strongly opposing the law.

【译】去年这项法律在州议会推进时，美国广告公司协会（简称4A's）和其他几个广告组织发表了强烈反对该法律的声明。

It said in a blog post that it would hurt advertisers by "injecting compliance uncertainty into the advertising process, imposing burdens on brands (and their agencies) advertising in New York, and undermining creative and technological innovation."

【译】该协会在一篇博文中表示，这将损害广告商的利益，因为"给广告流程注入了合规性不确定性，给在纽约投放广告的品牌（及其代理机构）带来负担，并且破坏了创意和技术创新。"

Other groups, like the New York State Broadcasters Association, said in public statements released as the legislation became law that they were grateful for certain exemptions created through amendments, but still had concerns about the broad definition of a synthetic performer.

【译】其他组织，如纽约州广播协会，在该立法成为法律的过程中发表的公开声明中表示，他们对通过修正案创建的某些豁免感到欣慰，但仍然对合成表演者的宽泛定义感到担忧。

The law's biggest supporter was the SAG-AFTRA actors' union, which recently approved a new contract with studios and streaming platforms that it says provides even more protections against synthetic performers.

【译】该法律的最大支持者是演员工会SAG-AFTRA，该工会最近与制片厂和流媒体平台批准了一项新合同，称该合同为进一步防范合成表演者提供了更多保护。

The law is one of many that have been proposed or enacted by states across the U.S. to increase job security for real people or rein in potential privacy and security risks posed by AI.

【译】这项法律是美国多个州提出或颁布的多项法律之一，其目的是提高真人的工作保障，或遏制人工智能带来的潜在隐私和安全风险。

Existing state laws that have passed include prohibiting deepfakes in certain contexts, limiting the collection of some personal data, and requiring more transparency from companies.

【译】已经通过的现有州法律包括禁止特定情况下的深度伪造、限制收集某些个人信息，以及要求企业提高透明度。

Just after Hochul signed the synthetic performers law in December, President Donald Trump signed an executive order pressuring states not to regulate AI. The move came from concerns that a patchwork of state regulations would stifle AI companies' growth and allow China to catch up with the U.S. in the AI race. Critics of the executive order argued it would allow tech companies to operate with virtually no oversight.

【译】就在霍楚尔去年12月签署合成表演者相关法律后，特朗普总统签署了一项行政命令，向各州施压，要求它们不要监管人工智能。此举是出于担心各州零散的监管会阻碍人工智能公司的增长，让中国在人工智能竞赛中赶上美国。该行政命令的批评者认为，这将使科技公司几乎可以在没有任何监督的情况下运营。
