Anthropic announces 'Claude Corps' to teach nonprofits to use AI more effectively

Anthropic 宣布推出"Claude Corps"项目，教非营利组织更有效使用 AI
Associated Press (AP News) Glenn Gamboa June 12, 2026

SAN FRANCISCO (AP) — Anthropic will donate $150 million to launch a fellowship program that places people early in their careers with nonprofits around the country to help them use artificial intelligence more effectively.

【译】旧金山（美联社）——Anthropic 将捐赠 1.5 亿美元推出一项奖学金项目，将职业生涯早期的从业者派驻到全国各地的非营利组织，帮助他们更有效地在工作中使用人工智能。

Claude Corps, named after the company's popular AI chatbot, will recruit and place 1,000 members trained in using Claude for one-year stints at various organizations. Anthropic President Daniela Amodei told The Associated Press in an interview that the company hopes it will expand and become a major pillar of its strategy to help humanity reap the benefits of AI while managing its risks.

【译】"Claude Corps"以该公司热门 AI 聊天机器人 Claude 命名，将招聘并派驻 1000 名接受过 Claude 使用培训的成员，到各类组织开展为期一年的工作。Anthropic 总裁丹妮拉·阿莫代伊（Daniela Amodei）在接受美联社采访时表示，公司希望该项目能够扩展，成为其帮助人类在享受 AI 益处的同时管控相关风险这一战略的重要支柱。

"We hope this is a good idea that can take root, that others can build off of and learn from, whether it's in the public sector or the private sector," Amodei said in an interview at Anthropic's headquarters in San Francisco. "But I think my expectation is that we will learn from it, the people who participate will learn from it, and we can come back and do better next time."

【译】"我们希望这是一个可以扎根的好想法，其他人可以据此构建并从中学习，无论是公共部门还是私营部门，"阿莫代伊在旧金山的 Anthropic 总部接受采访时说，"但我想我的期望是，我们会从中学习，参与这个项目的人也会学习，我们下次可以回来做得更好。"

Anthropic's commitment includes paying the Claude Corps members a salary and providing at least 400 partner organizations with $10,000 grants and credits to use Claude for free.

【译】Anthropic 的承诺包括向 Claude Corps 成员支付薪酬，并且向至少 400 家合作机构提供 1 万美元的资助以及免费使用 Claude 的额度。

BALANCING PROFIT AND SOCIAL IMPACT
平衡利润与社会影响

Amodei said philanthropy has been woven into the way the co-founders run the company. She, her brother and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, and five other co-founders of the company have already pledged to give away 80% of their wealth. They set up Anthropic as a public benefit corporation, a designation available to for-profit companies that choose to balance financial goals and social impact.

【译】阿莫代伊表示，慈善理念已经融入 Anthropic 联合创始人运营公司的思路中。阿莫代伊、她的哥哥、Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代伊（Dario Amodei）以及公司的另外五位联合创始人已经承诺，将捐赠他们 80% 的财富。他们将 Anthropic 设立为公益公司，这是一种营利性公司可以选择的法律形式，用来平衡财务目标和社会影响。

Anthropic, currently valued at $965 billion, is preparing to go public on Wall Street, announcing earlier this month that it had filed confidential paperwork for an initial public offering.

【译】Anthropic 目前的估值为 9650 亿美元，正准备在华尔街上市，本月早些时候宣布已提交首次公开募股（IPO）的保密申请文件。

"We may make some decisions and choices that could come into conflict with what's in the pure commercial interest of the business, and we'll be very open about that," she said. "I think it serves us well to be very honest about who we are."

【译】"我们可能会做出一些决策和选择，这些决策和选择可能与企业的纯商业利益相冲突，我们会非常坦诚地对待这一点，"她说，"我认为，对我们来说，非常诚实地展示我们的身份对我们很有利。"

RISKS AND REGULATION
风险与监管

Anthropic has been vocal about the risks inherent in the groundbreaking technology. The company warned last week that companies should coordinate to pause advanced AI development if humanity faces the risk of losing control of self-improving technology.

【译】Anthropic 一直直言不讳地指出这项突破性技术固有的风险。该公司上周警告称，如果人类面临失去对自我改进技术控制的风险，各公司应该协调暂停先进 AI 系统的开发。

It also partnered with Pope Leo XIV to help draft an encyclical on AI and the need for greater regulation, and has had a high-profile clash with the Trump administration over Anthropic's refusal to give the U.S. military unfettered access to its AI technology.

【译】该公司还与教皇良十四世合作，协助后者制定关于 AI 以及加强监管必要性的通谕。此外，Anthropic 还曾与特朗普政府发生备受关注的冲突，原因是 Anthropic 拒绝允许美国军方不受限制地使用其 AI 技术。

Bella DeVaan, director of the charitable reform initiative at the Institute for Policy Studies, a progressive research group, said she doubted any AI company would voluntarily set aside enough profits to support all the people affected by the proliferation of AI.

【译】进步研究组织政策研究所慈善改革倡议主任贝拉·德瓦安（Bella DeVaan）表示，她怀疑任何 AI 公司都不会自愿拨出足够的利润，来支持所有受 AI 普及影响的人。

"The fox cannot guard the henhouse," said DeVaan, who studies giving by the super-wealthy. "They cannot be in charge of their own regulation, and they cannot define what their philanthropic mission is. That has to be decided by the public."

【译】"狐狸不能看守鸡舍，"研究超级富豪捐赠的德瓦安说，"他们不能负责自己的监管，也不能自己定义他们的慈善使命是什么。这必须由公众来决定。"

HOW CLAUDE CORPS WILL WORK
Claude Corps 如何运作

To create Claude Corps, Anthropic is partnering with CodePath, a San Francisco-based nonprofit that helps first-generation college students and low-income students enter tech careers through higher education courses and career support.

【译】为了创建 Claude Corps，Anthropic 与 CodePath 合作，后者是总部位于旧金山的非营利组织，旨在通过高等教育课程和职业支持，帮助第一代大学生和低收入学生进入科技行业工作。

Michael Ellison, CEO of CodePath, said he has long been thinking about reimagining AmeriCorps for the proliferation of AI. "I think we need programs like this that can plug into the populations that traditionally adopt new technology later," he said.

【译】CodePath 首席执行官迈克尔·埃利森（Michael Ellison）表示，他长期以来一直在考虑重新设计美国志愿队（AmeriCorps），以适应 AI 的普及。"我认为我们需要这样的项目，能够对接那些传统上较晚采用新技术的群体，"他说。

The fellowship will be open to a broad range of people early in their careers. "We're intentionally making it as easy as possible to apply," Ellison said. "We don't require that you have a specific degree. We want this first cohort to be representative of the broad swath of people in this country."

【译】该奖学金将面向广泛的职业生涯早期的年轻人开放。"我们有意让申请尽可能容易，"埃利森说，"我们不要求你必须有特定的学位。我们希望首批奖学金获得者能够代表这个国家广泛的人群。"

Jennifer Blatz, CEO and president of StriveTogether, a Cincinnati-based nonprofit network, said her organization was honored to be selected to receive two Claude Corps fellows.

【译】总部位于辛辛那提的非营利网络 StriveTogether 的首席执行官兼总裁詹妮弗·布拉茨（Jennifer Blatz）表示，她的组织很荣幸被选为 Claude Corps 奖学金的接收方，将接收两名成员。

"AI can help us work smarter, but building trust and community collaboration is a very human part of the work," she said. "That doesn't go away because we're using this tool."

【译】"AI 可以帮助我们更聪明地工作，但建立信任和社区协作，是工作中非常人性化的一部分，"她说，"这不会因为我们使用这个工具就消失。"
