Microsoft cuts 4,800 jobs, including many at Xbox in a 'reset' of its gaming division

微软裁员4800人，包括大量Xbox员工，对其游戏部门进行"重置"
Associated Press (AP News) July 6, 2026
THE LAYOFFS
裁员情况

REDMOND, Wash. (AP) — Microsoft is cutting 4,800 jobs, about 2.1% of its global workforce, including a large number of workers at its Xbox video game business.

【译】雷德蒙德，华盛顿州（美联社）——微软正在裁员4800人，约占其全球员工的2.1%，其中包括大量Xbox视频游戏业务的员工。

The layoffs included 1,600 Xbox workers, with more to come this year in a broader reorganization designed to "reset" Xbox as it faces heightened competition, the company said Monday.

【译】此次裁员包括1600名Xbox员工，且今年还会有更多裁员——这是旨在"重置"Xbox以应对日益激烈竞争的更大规模重组的一部分，该公司周一表示。

XBOX CEO'S MEMO
Xbox CEO的内部备忘录

"Our business today is not healthy," said a memo from Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, who took over the gaming division earlier this year. "We are operating at margins that are 3-10x lower than comparable platform and publishing businesses."

【译】今年早些时候接管游戏部门的Xbox首席执行官阿莎·夏尔马（Asha Sharma）在一份内部备忘录中表示："我们目前的业务并不健康。我们的利润率比同类平台和出版业务低了3到10倍。"

Sharma said the industry, in which Xbox competes with Sony's PlayStation and Nintendo's Switch, is facing a severe "hardware crisis" as costs soar for console components.

【译】夏尔马表示，Xbox与索尼的PlayStation和任天堂的Switch竞争的游戏行业正面临严重的"硬件危机"，因为游戏机组件的成本大幅飙升。

FURTHER CUTS AND DIVESTITURES
进一步裁员与剥离工作室

Beyond the layoffs announced Monday, Sharma said Xbox expects another 1,600 job cuts over the course of the fiscal year that began last week. The company is also spinning off four video game development studios previously acquired by Microsoft.

【译】除了周一宣布的裁员之外，夏尔马表示Xbox预计在上周开始的这个财年内还将再裁员1600人。公司还将剥离此前由微软收购的四家视频游戏开发工作室。

THE ACTIVISION DEAL

Nearly three years ago, Microsoft closed a $69 billion deal to acquire gaming giant Activision Blizzard, maker of "Call of Duty" and other blockbuster franchises. The company said at the time it wanted to broaden its game development portfolio and offer a Netflix-like streaming subscription service, but the strategy does not appear to have been enough to get ahead of the competition.

【译】近三年前，微软完成了以690亿美元收购游戏巨头动视暴雪（Activision Blizzard）的交易——动视暴雪是《使命召唤》（Call of Duty）及其他重量级游戏系列的开发商。微软当时表示，希望扩大其游戏开发组合，并提供类似Netflix的流媒体订阅服务，但这一战略似乎不足以让微软在竞争中取得领先。

"While those businesses have created meaningful value, they did not grow at the pace we expected," Sharma said.

【译】"虽然这些业务创造了有意义的价值，但它们并没有以我们预期的速度增长。"夏尔马说。

BROADER MICROSOFT LAYOFFS
微软整体裁员

The Xbox cuts are in addition to broader Microsoft layoffs that the software giant's chief people officer Amy Coleman tied to unspecified changes in customer needs.

【译】Xbox的裁员是在微软更广泛的裁员基础上进行的——这家软件巨头的人力总监艾米·科尔曼（Amy Coleman）将裁员与客户需求的不明确变化联系在一起。

"I also want to be direct that the roles eliminated today are not being replaced by AI," Coleman wrote in a blog post.

【译】"我也要直接说明：今天被裁掉的岗位并不是被AI取代的。"科尔曼在一篇博客文章中写道。

The layoffs followed voluntary buyouts that Microsoft began offering to about 8,750 people in May. More than 30% of eligible workers accepted those voluntary retirement offers, Coleman said Monday.

【译】此次裁员之前，微软已于5月开始向约8750名员工提供自愿离职补偿方案。科尔曼周一表示，超过30%的符合条件的员工接受了自愿退休提议。
